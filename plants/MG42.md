### Original Notes from Google Doc
MG42

another update - took the longest shower in history. this is heady af!! this is the sativa leaner. totally unexpected but really really good if smoked as a sativa. the parents make you expect a heavy indica high but this is certainly not. my mind is racing happily.

update - yes, really really nice. relaxes the back after planting 100 grapevines, but not overly strong. very gassy nose. i like it more than mule fuel but less than Nana Glue. I think i wanna go light up the rest of the joint. probably not the one since im barely 1 whiskey deep and can sense i need more mind and body relaxation.

reaay nice high. one of the best i’m pretty sure. not overly strong, but plenty powerful. very lovely warm enveloping high. 

High stretch, minimal leaves, minimal purple nice tight nugs with a fruity gassy smell. Above average appeal.

Mule Fuel type pheno but with a gassy fruity smell. interesting at day 32.




Day 42:
 
Smells are intensified and still extremely pleasant and unexpected. The epoxy smell with fruity back end. Mule Fuel morphology is prominent but with Nana Glue’s stretch. So far the stems can hold up the weight of the buds without support.
